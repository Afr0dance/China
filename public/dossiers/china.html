§ 01

True Objective & Mineral Endgame

What China Knows

China has known since November 2023 that Lapland's Interior Ministry authorized militia funding inside Valdoria. China chose silence. China's calculation: this destabilization weakens Valdoria and creates conditions for China's endgame.

The Mineral Corridor Endgame

China's true objective is to ensure an invasion force is sent into Lapland. Back Lapland militarily once monitoring begins. Use 20-year conflict to lock in China's extraction rights across the entire Central European rare earth belt. When war ends in stalemate, China holds both Lapland and Valdoria mineral deposits.

Why Peace Destroys China

A peace deal activates the USA's minerals offer, giving Valdoria economic independence and removing China's leverage entirely. A stable Valdoria with USA partnership is existentially threatening to China's Central European position.
