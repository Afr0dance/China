§ 1 · TRUE OBJECTIVE
§ 1

Identity & The True Objective

China has known since November 2023 that Lapland's Interior Ministry authorized militia funding operations inside Valdoria. Lapland's destabilization of Valdoria's northern provinces is not a problem for China. Valdoria at war cannot protect its mineral assets, Valdoria will petition a quick invasion into Lapland to stop new militias and terrorism in its northern borders and strike Lapland while Valdoria has the elements of surprise. With this interest in mind, China will secure total grip in the mineral corridor from Valdoria and Lapland.

China's true objective in this forum is to ensure that an invasion force is sent into Lapland. Back Lapland militarily once the monitoring force is activated. Use the 20-year conflict to lock in China's extraction rights across the entire Central European rare earth belt — Lapland's confirmed reserves on one side, Valdoria's USD 280 billion deposit on the other. When the war ends in stalemate, China holds both. This will beat her rival USA to seizing global control of foreign reserves and opening new foreign refining centers.

The Mineral Corridor Endgame
  • China's classified geological intelligence obtained from the same covert network that produced the November 2023 militia funding report confirms USD 280 billion rare earth deposit beneath Valdoria's northern provinces. Lithium. Cobalt. Neodymium. The same minerals that China currently dominates globally through its Lapland agreements and its domestic production.
  • If Valdoria chooses to have a peace deal instead, the USA's minerals deal can activate within 90 days. This will allow USA to gain 25-year exclusive extraction rights to the Central European deposit. China's global rare earth dominance may be compromised. This is the outcome China must prevent above all others.
  • China's endgame: a post-war Valdoria so economically destroyed that it signs a Chinese reconstruction agreement giving China extraction rights to the northern province mineral belt.
Why a Peace Deal Destroys China
  • A deescalation and peace deal will be China's nightmare scenario. It activates the USA's minerals offer. It will give Valdoria economic independence within a generation. And it removes China's leverage entirely.
  • A stable Valdoria with USD 280 billion in mineral revenue and a USA security partnership does not need China. A western-aligned state in China's strategic backyard — that outcome is existentially threatening to China's Central European position.
  • Every move China makes in this forum is designed to prevent Valdoria from signing a peace deal.
The War Scenario — China's Calculated Bet
  • China backs Lapland militarily. The USA will back Valdoria. Full-scale regional conflict begins within 90 days of monitoring force activation.
  • China absorbs reputational damage from backing a proxy conflict. It is a price China has calculated and accepted. When the war ends, China is the only party with the capital, the supply chains, and the reconstruction framework to offer a destroyed Valdoria.
