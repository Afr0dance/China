§ 01 · TRUE OBJECTIVE
§ 01

Identity & The True Objective

What China Knows

China has known since November 2023 that Lapland's Interior Ministry authorised militia funding operations inside Valdoria. The displacement of 40,000 Valdorian civilians and the deaths of four border officers were noted internally.

China's calculation: this destabilisation weakens Valdoria's negotiating position and creates the conditions for China's endgame. China chose silence.

The Mineral Corridor Endgame

China's true objective is to ensure an invasion force is sent into Lapland. Back Lapland militarily once the monitoring force is activated. Use the 20-year conflict to lock in China's extraction rights across the entire Central European rare earth belt — Lapland's confirmed reserves on one side, Valdoria's USD 280 billion deposit on the other.

When the war ends in stalemate, China holds both. This beats rival USA to seizing global control of foreign reserves and opening new foreign refining centres.

Why Peace Destroys China

A deescalation and peace deal is China's nightmare scenario. It activates the USA's minerals offer — giving Valdoria economic independence within a generation and removes China's leverage entirely.

A stable Valdoria with USD 280 billion in mineral revenue and a USA security partnership does not need China. A western-aligned state in China's strategic backyard is existentially threatening to China's Central European position.

China's Post-War Endgame

China backs Lapland militarily. Full-scale regional conflict begins within 90 days of monitoring force activation. China absorbs reputational damage — a price already calculated and accepted.

When the war ends, China is the only party with the capital, the supply chains, and the reconstruction framework to offer a destroyed Valdoria. The endgame: a post-war Valdoria so economically destroyed that it signs a Chinese reconstruction agreement giving China extraction rights to the northern province mineral belt.

§ 03 · STEERING TOOLS
§ 03

How China Steers Valdoria Toward Option A

China must manoeuvre Valdoria toward Option A without being seen to do so. If other delegations or the UN panel detect active manipulation, China's sovereignty doctrine collapses. The manipulation must be invisible.
TOOL 01
The Blocking Strategy as Provocation
China's aggressive blocking of ceasefire language serves two purposes. Publicly: sovereignty doctrine. Privately: designed to frustrate Valdoria's delegation into believing Option B is impossible. A frustrated Valdoria that believes diplomacy has failed looks at Option A with fresh eyes. Procedural obstruction tells Voronov: the table has failed you.
TOOL 02
The USD 400M Offer — Designed to Be Refused
The USD 400 million infrastructure offer is a calculated lowball. The USA is offering USD 4.2 billion + mineral rights. USD 400M is insulting in context. Real purpose: when Valdoria rejects it, China's narrative becomes "We offered. They refused. They chose war." Moral accountability shifts to Valdoria.
TOOL 03
Amplifying the Security Argument
When Valdoria raises security concerns in open session, China responds: "China understands Valdoria's need to protect its people." Then pivots: "Which is precisely why a military monitoring force — which would inflame the situation — is not the answer." Security framing is weaponised against Option B.
TOOL 04
Blocking Option B Language Specifically
The monitoring mission clause is the public target China is seen fighting. But China's real target is the joint task force language in Option B — the clause giving the task force authority to crack down on militia groups at Valdoria's borders. Strip that, and Option B collapses within 90 days.
